# Decisions

## Autonomous decisions

During autonomous work, resolve a genuine choice with the recommended option and record it in the governing artifact as `Assumption: chosen without user review`, naming the rejected alternative and the accepted trade-off. Report the artifact's assumption count. Never delegate asking the user.

## One-way and two-way doors

- A **two-way door** reverses within acceptable time and cost, and its consequences stay contained meanwhile. Pick the best option with one short reason. No scorecard.
- A **one-way door** is impossible, slow, or expensive to reverse, or can cause material legal, financial, safety, data, or external harm before reversal. Treat uncertain reversibility as one-way. Before scoring options, fix 3 to 7 independent criteria, with Reversibility and Risk weighted highest; score every option with evidence and state what facts would reopen the decision.

When the caller names a decision owner other than itself and the choice is a one-way door, return the framed choice, the options, and the classification. Do not pick.

## Decision records

A decision record states the context, the decision in active voice, the rejected alternatives, and the consequences that become easier and harder.
